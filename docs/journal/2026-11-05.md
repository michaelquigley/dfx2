- **The supervisor's frame-timer decision ordering is load-bearing** (`scheduleFrameLocked`): an armed timer keeps its deadline even once past, and only a deadline first seen already due wakes directly. The first version stopped any timer whose deadline had passed, which loses the frame when the supervisor runs late with no dispatch pending; a rendering frame's own `pendingLocked` check masks this in most schedules, so an end-to-end test could not discriminate. `TestLateDeadlineEvaluationNeverDropsTheFrame` exercises the decision directly and fails against the old ordering.

## Origin: a study of mygo, and the first text spike

dfx2 grew out of a session in a local clone of mygo (`~/Sandbox/mygo`, at
`da6e0fbf`). The session studied it next to dfx and dfw. Michael ruled out
switching to mygo and ruled out borrowing its code: "we can rebuild it if the
ideas are useful." Treat `docs/future/mygo-study.md` as a catalog of
mechanisms to weigh, not of code to port.

The session's decisions are recorded with their reasons in
`docs/future/direction.md`, and the text work in `docs/future/text.md`. They
aren't repeated here.

The `dfx2` roadmap card was captured in dfx and moved here when the
repository was created. Its frontmatter (`researching`, milestone `v0.2.x`)
is Michael's from dfx's board.
